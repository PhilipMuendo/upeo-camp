# Image credits & replacement guide

All photography in `/assets/images` is **placeholder stock** from Unsplash and Pexels (free licences), graded to one
golden-hour film look with `scripts/grade.py`. Wildlife photos are from East Africa but **not** from a real Upeo
camp, and the tent interiors are not of a real property.

**Before launch, replace them with the camp's own photography** (tents, mess, guides, vehicles, real sightings).
Keep the same filename to swap in place, then run `python scripts/grade.py <raw-photos> assets/images` to match the grade.
